Seoul Subway Day PlannerAdd your stops in order and how long you'll stay. We add up the subway time so you can see your whole day.

Subway times are estimates: about 2 minutes per stop on most lines, plus 5 minutes for each transfer (walking between platforms and waiting). Routes prefer fewer transfers. Times lean a little long on purpose: better to arrive early than late. Some big stations (like Jamsil on Line 8) have long walks to the exit, so give yourself extra time there. Your plan stays in this browser.
